<section class="entry" id="ruledep"><h3>RuleDep · Dependency-aware rule aggregation</h3><div class="meta">2025.08–2026.06 · ICDE 2027 · First author · Accepted</div><ul class="project-points"><li><strong>Project Description:</strong> Models complementarity and redundancy among rules using sparse second-order corrections and two-stage training.
