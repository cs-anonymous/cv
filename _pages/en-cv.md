<section class="entry" id="expath"><h3>eXpath: Explaining Knowledge Graph Link Prediction with Ontological Closed Path Rules (PVLDB / VLDB 2025, CCF-A)</h3><div class="meta">2024.01–2025.01 · First author · Accepted</div><ul class="project-points"><li>Addresses the local and semantically weak explanations of embedding-based knowledge graph link prediction by combining relational paths with ontological closed-path rules.</li><li>Designs a path aggregation, rule mining, and critical-fact selection pipeline: bidirectional BFS searches paths up to three hops, compresses them into relation paths, and mines Closed Path (CP) and Property Transition (PT) rules to rank explanatory facts.</li><li>Can be fused with single-link explainers such as Kelpie; across benchmark datasets and link-prediction models, improves explanation-quality metrics by about 20% and reduces explanation time by 61.4%.</li></ul><div class="entry-links"><a href="https://github.com/cs-anonymous/eXpath">Code ↗</a></div></section>
